# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our Pledge

We want PARAMANIACS to be a friendly, welcoming and harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, experience level, background, nationality, gender identity and expression, disability, appearance, religion or any other personal characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and respectful
- Welcoming beginners and answering their questions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Discrimination or offensive jokes targeting people
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **your-email@example.com** (replace with your contact address). All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with respect for the privacy of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps, depending on the severity of the behavior:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning with an explanation.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban for a pattern of violations or serious harm.
